Right to work (Home Office)

Before employment starts

  • Carry out right-to-work checks for every employee before work starts; keep records and follow-up checks for time-limited permission.
  • Do not employ a person who does not have the right to do the work offered.
  • Use compliant check methods (original documents or Home Office online check/share code where applicable).
Manage employee RTW records

DBS checks

Role/site dependent

  • DBS checks are not universally mandatory for all cleaners; they are required/permitted for eligible roles, especially work with children/vulnerable adults or specific regulated sites.
  • Record DBS status, certificate/reference, issue/renewal dates only where appropriate and lawful.
  • Follow data protection rules: keep only what is necessary and retain securely.

Health & Safety / COSHH / PPE

HSE cleaning business duties

  • Provide H&S induction, training, supervision, and risk assessments for cleaning tasks.
  • For chemicals: complete COSHH assessments, keep SDS accessible, label containers, store safely, and train staff.
  • Provide suitable PPE and maintain equipment. Record training/PPE/risk assessment acknowledgements.
Stock/COSHH Equipment

Equipment safety (PUWER / Electrical)

Vacuums, scrubbers, electrical machines

  • PUWER: equipment must be suitable, safe, maintained, inspected where risk requires, and used by trained people.
  • Electrical equipment should be maintained; inspect visually and carry out PAT/testing where appropriate by risk assessment.
  • Quarantine unsafe equipment until repaired/replaced. Keep service/inspection records.
